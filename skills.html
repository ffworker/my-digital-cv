Self-assessed · intentionally honest

What I know, where I still need help, and what I would trust myself to change.

The numbers below are my own assessment. The more important part is next to them: how independently I work, where I lean on docs or AI, and how careful I would be before touching production.

How I use AI: mostly as a speed multiplier, a second pair of eyes, or a way to close logic gaps. Where I depend on it heavily, I say so. If I cannot explain a change well enough to verify it, it belongs in a lab first.
