ONE VERY ORDINARY EXAMPLE

The server nobody remembers.

When an old server has no clear owner or documentation, I don't just delete it. I shut it down in a controlled way, watch for consequences over the following days and keep the backup as a way back. Sometimes the most useful change is knowing what not to touch yet.

My rule of thumb

Understand it first. Keep a way back. Write down what the next person should know.
