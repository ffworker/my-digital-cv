What I know, where I still need help, and what I would trust myself to change.

The numbers are my own assessment. More important is the context beside them: how independently I work, where I use documentation or AI, and how cautious I would be before touching a production system.

How I use AI: mostly as a speed multiplier, second opinion, or a way to find reasoning gaps faster. Where I depend on it heavily, I say so. If I cannot explain and verify a change, it belongs in the lab first.
