Containerized services are part of the home lab, especially where deployment, updates and recovery matter more than novelty.

Monitoring turns the lab from "probably running" into something that can be checked, debugged and improved.

  • Grafana dashboards for visibility
  • InfluxDB 1.8 for time-series storage in existing setups
  • service checks and logs used as the truth source during troubleshooting
deployed systems

raspberry-pi-fleet / physical-lab

Three Raspberry Pi systems provide the grounding layer: a mixed-service Pi 4, a watering automation Pi 3 and a public camera Pi Zero W.

  • Pi 4: camera controller, file services, Paperless-ngx, Portainer, Node-RED, Grafana and InfluxDB
  • Pi 3: DHT22/hygrometer-based watering automation
  • Pi Zero W: lightweight public surveillance camera exercise
