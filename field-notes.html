FIELD NOTES · IT OPERATIONS

Field notes from IT operations

Some of the most valuable lessons in IT do not come from routine maintenance, but from unusual situations that demand sound judgment, careful troubleshooting and clear communication. This is where I intend to reflect on those lessons — without exposing the people or organisations involved.

Field Notes now lives on the Bytegeist Blog. Project stories appear there; this page keeps the confidentiality guidelines. Visit the blog ↗

What these notes will focus on

Decisions under pressure

How priorities, technical risk and communication influence the response to an unexpected situation.

Troubleshooting that matters

What was observed, which assumptions needed checking and how a result was validated.

Lessons worth keeping

How a difficult day can inform safer processes, documentation and future decisions.

Confidentiality is non-negotiable

I do not publish employer or customer identifiers, precise timelines, infrastructure details, security indicators, screenshots, internal communications or information that could be traced back to a specific organisation. Anonymisation alone does not override confidentiality or employer obligations. Any real case needs a separate review before publication.
