Self-assessed · intentionally transparent

What I can do myself, where I use help, and where I slow down.

These are my own ratings, not certification scores. A high number means I can usually reason through the work myself. A lower number means I am still learning, often with documentation, search, or AI assistance. The important part is knowing the difference before touching a real system.

How I use assistance: I use documentation, search and AI when they make me faster, but I do not treat generated output as understanding. For areas where my manual confidence is low, I verify changes in a disposable lab or sandbox first. Public repositories are linked where they are useful evidence; private operational configuration stays private.
Self-rating

How independently can I work?

10 means deeply familiar in day-to-day work; lower scores mean narrower experience or active learning.

Assistance use

What do I reach for?

Memory, documentation, search, or AI — and whether assistance is convenience or a current dependency.

Production posture

How cautious am I?

Independent where I understand the failure modes; review- or sandbox-first where I do not.
