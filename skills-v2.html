Self-assessed · deliberately explicit

What I can do myself, where I use help, and how cautious I am in production.

A skill list is only useful if it says something about depth. This page separates manual confidence from tool assistance and production confidence instead of pretending every technology on a CV is equally strong.

Important: AI/search/docs assistance is not presented as competence by itself. If I rely on assistance for a task, I still need to understand the change well enough to verify it. Anything I cannot confidently reason about goes through a lab or sandbox before I would use it on a real system.
Manual confidence

How far can I get without generated instructions?

Examples: independent, comfortable with docs, basic familiarity, or learning.

Assistance use

What do I normally lean on?

Examples: memory, documentation/reference, search, or AI-assisted drafting/troubleshooting.

Production posture

How would I use it on a real system?

Examples: independent change, peer/change review, or sandbox-first before touching production.
